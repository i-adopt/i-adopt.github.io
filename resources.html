Author

Barbara Magagna

Published

October 13, 2026

I-ADOPT related Specifications

  • June 2021: First public release of the I-ADOPT ontology 0.0.8
  • July 2023: Improved version I-ADOPT ontology 1.0.1
  • May 2025: Major revision I-ADOPT ontology 1.1.0
  • 2026: I-ADOPT SHACL Shape definitions
  • 2026: Variable Design Patterns and Decision Trees
  • 2026: I-ADOPT Guideline
  • 2026: I-ADOPT Corpus

I-ADOPT related Services

  • 2025: A Visualizer enabling interactive modelling and visualizing of an I-ADOPT variable
  • 2025: I-ADOPT Catalogue
  • 2026: A Tool for Variable Descriptions for local installation
  • 2026: Access the Service online: Visualize, edit, prepare, and export Turtle without an account. For LLM-enabled variable decomposition login is required (only by invitation).
